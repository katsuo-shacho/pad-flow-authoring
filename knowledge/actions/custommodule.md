# Custom actions in actions reference section

出典: https://learn.microsoft.com/ja-jp/power-automate/desktop-flows/actions-reference/custommodule （Microsoft Docs, CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ — 本プロジェクトによる要約・圧縮。Microsoft の見解ではない。LEGAL.md 参照）

概要: Custom actions developed by your organization and uploaded to the respective environments can be included in desktop flows and utilized like actions that belong in the standard library of automation actions. Custom actions exist at the environment level. As a best practice, use a "dev&mdash;test&mdash;prod" model when developing custom actions.
